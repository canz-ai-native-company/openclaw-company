### 2026 landing-page / CRO / lead-gen trends for aesthetic+wellness
- Mobile-first responsive (>70% mobile traffic), thumb-friendly CTAs, click-to-call
- Soft, empathetic aesthetics (muted nature palettes, rounded shapes, gentle gradients, real-life diverse photography)
- Trust signals = before/after with disclaimers, real unscripted testimonials, provider credentials
- Per-service landing pages (e.g., "Semaglutide in Dallas") outperform generic homepages for SEO + paid traffic
- Top-tier aesthetic clinic conversion rate ≈ **11%**; industry avg ≈ 2.35%
- 93% of patients check reviews before booking
- AI receptionist + automated reminders + SMS sequences → 20–30% lift on conversion
- "Refreshed, not done" — natural-results narrative outperforms transformation maximalism
