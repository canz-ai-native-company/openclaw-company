Executive Summary

CRO audit of 4 US-based aesthetic & wellness clinics offering medical weight loss (GLP-1 and lipotropic options) was completed to assess messaging clarity, value proposition, CTA effectiveness, social proof, and content quality. The aggregate Content Effectiveness Index (CEI) across sites is 4.5/10. Messaging clarity scores range up to 8/10 but CTA effectiveness remains markedly under-optimized (average 2.25/10). Social proof shows wide dispersion (1–7/10) and content quality sits mid-range (3–6/10). The data point to a cross-site gap in conversion architecture, with the highest leverage coming from (a) CTA optimization, (b) visible social proof, and (c) pricing transparency. The primary opportunity is to implement conversion-focused CTAs paired with credible social proof and clear pricing to align buyer intent with the on-site path to schedule a visit or engagement.
