## 4. KEY MARKET INSIGHTS

### Target Audience Profile
**Primary:** Suburban Houston parents, ages 28–50, managing dental care for families of 3–5
- Dual-income households with children in school
- Time-starved; scheduling friction is a conversion killer
- Live in master-planned communities (Katy, Pearland, Sugar Land, Cypress, The Woodlands)
- Active on Facebook, Instagram, Nextdoor
- Trust neighbors' recommendations (Nextdoor, local Facebook groups) more than ads
- Influenced by Google reviews (4.8+ star threshold)
- Price-conscious but will pay more for trust and convenience

**Secondary:** Seniors and adults who have delayed dental care (the "dental avoider" segment)
- Driven by dental anxiety, cost uncertainty, or bad past experiences
- Respond to judgment-free, gentle dentistry messaging

### Customer Pain Points (Evidence-Based)
1. **Dental anxiety** — 36% of US population affected; primary barrier to first appointment
2. **Fear of surprise costs** — patients report being charged for services not discussed beforehand
3. **Feeling rushed** — "assembly line" criticism is common in negative reviews
4. **Not being heard** — patients feel dentists don't explain treatment or address concerns
5. **Inconvenient scheduling** — many practices are Mon–Fri only; suburban families need Saturday/evening options
6. **Insurance confusion** — patients don't know if their plan is accepted until they call
7. **Long wait times** — both for appointments and in-office
8. **Fear of judgment** — patients embarrassed about dental neglect avoid seeking care

### Buying Triggers
1. Recent move to suburb (new residents actively seeking a family dentist)
2. Child's first dental appointment
3. Pain or dental emergency
4. Insurance change or coverage expiration notice
5. Dissatisfaction with previous dentist (rushed, unexplained costs, poor experience)
6. Neighbor/friend recommendation
7. Google "dentist near me" search on mobile
8. Seasonal (back-to-school, new year's benefit reset)

### Trust Factors That Convert
- 4.8+ Google star rating with 100+ reviews (threshold for credibility)
- Real photos of the team and office (not stock)
- Doctor credentials/bio prominently featured
- Specific insurance plans listed (not "most insurance")
- Before/after case photos (cosmetic/restorative work)
- Clear new patient special with all inclusions listed
- Online booking available 24/7 (patients book after hours)
- Response to reviews (shows engaged practice)
- Specific suburb-naming in copy ("serving Katy families since...")

### Proof Opportunities for Sunrise
- Feature real team members with warm bios and photos
- Patient before/after photos (get releases immediately after launch)
- Curated Google/Facebook review pulls
- "New patients welcome" badge with specific offer details
- Insurance logos (specific carriers)
- Video testimonials (short, 30–60 seconds)
- Community involvement photos (school events, sponsorships)
