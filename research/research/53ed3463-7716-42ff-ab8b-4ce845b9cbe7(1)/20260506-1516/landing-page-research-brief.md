## 4. Key market insights

- **Pains:** unmeasured CPL/CPA, weak CTAs, no-shows (~30%, $500–$700/visit lost), social proof not reused, telehealth absent, multi-market expansion blocked by agency dependency.
- **Objections clinic owners voice:** "Another agency?" "What about HIPAA?" "Will I lose my Google history?" "My current agency owns the ad account." "How is this different from $999 packages?"
- **Buying triggers:** GLP-1 demand wave they can't capture, multi-market ambition, board pressure for unit economics, an agency contract ending, a no-show crisis.
- **Trust factors:** real dashboards, named operators, HIPAA + BAA, ad-account ownership, exit clauses, third-party benchmarks (Goldman –41.6% CPL, ADA 30–50% no-show reduction).
- **Proof opportunities:** anonymized case bars from MD/VA/NY clusters, $-attributed-to-reviews tickers, before/after CPL charts, dashboard screenshots, named operator quotes.

## 7. Final recommendation

**Ship Variation 1 — "The CPL Killer" — first.** It maps cleanest to the strongest Radar finding (0% of competitor clinics expose lead-gen metrics), differentiates against every direct competitor (none leads with measurable CPL guarantee), is the most performance-friendly hero for paid traffic, and naturally upsells into Variation 5 — "The Operator's OS" — for multi-location accounts.

**First test cycle (2-week sprint):**

- Hero subhead A/B: "predictable CPL/CPA dashboard" vs. "every booked patient, attributed to a dollar"
- Pricing block A/B: 3-tier vs. flat $2,495/mo
- CTA: "See your CPL forecast" vs. "Book a 20-min audit"
- Proof block: anonymized case bars vs. 60-second operator video

**Roll-out order:** Var 1 (paid) → Var 5 (outbound) → Var 4 (SEO/content) → Var 2 (front-desk-tool comparison ads) → Var 3 (partnerships/events).
