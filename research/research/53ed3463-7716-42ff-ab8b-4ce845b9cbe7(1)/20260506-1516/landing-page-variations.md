# LeadForge — 5 Landing Page Variations

**Audience:** Owners / medical directors / marketing leads of US medical weight loss & aesthetic clinics ($400–$1,200/mo programs).
**Promise frame:** Predictable, measured patient flow + AI booking + reputation engine for GLP-1 era clinics.
**Compliance frame:** HIPAA-aware copy. Avoid disease claims; speak about lead-flow, bookings, and operational outcomes — not medication outcomes.

Each variation is one coherent page (color, type, motion, copy, CTA all in sync). All 11+ sections must ship together — do not mix variations.

---

## VARIATION 1 — "The CPL Killer"

**Core hook:** Cut your cost per booked patient in half — and prove it on a dashboard.
**Angle:** Numerical, ROI-led, agency-replacement.
**Best for:** Operators burned by past agencies / paying $5K–$20K/mo with no clarity.

### Hero

- **Headline:** Stop paying for clicks. Start paying per booked patient.
- **Subhead:** LeadForge is the patient-acquisition engine that turns GLP-1 ad spend into a predictable CPL, CPA, and revenue dashboard — not another Slack channel of screenshots.
- **Primary CTA:** See your CPL forecast — book a 20-min audit
- **Secondary CTA:** Watch a 90-second demo
- **Visual:** Animated dashboard with numbers ticking down ($142 CPL → $71 CPL) under a counter for "patients booked this month."

### Section-by-section

1. **Hero** — see above.
2. **Trust strip** — "Built for clinics already running $20K+/mo in Meta & Google ad spend." Stat row: $24B GLP-1 market · 30M Americans on GLP-1 · $75 industry-avg Meta CPL · $125 Google healthcare CPL — *we beat both.*
3. **The problem** — "Your CPL chart looks like a stairstep. Up." Three pain bullets: agency reports you can't audit, no-shows eating 30%+ of bookings, ad fatigue every 14 days.
4. **The promise** — Three-tile outcome block: ⬇ CPL by 25–40% in 60 days · ⬆ booked-patient rate by 2× via AI front desk · ⬇ no-shows by 30–50% via automated recovery sequences. Each tile cites the source benchmark (Goldman Marketing case data, ADA reminder study).
5. **Unique mechanism — "The Forge Loop"** — Diagram with 5 nodes: Capture (LP + form) → Qualify (AI intake) → Book (calendar lock + deposit) → Recover (no-show ladder) → Reconvert (reviews → ad creative). Every node shows the metric it owns.
6. **How it works** — Step list with timelines. Day 1: audit + tracking install. Day 7: ads live with temperature segmentation. Day 14: AI booking flow live. Day 30: first CPL report. Day 60: optimization milestone.
7. **Live dashboard mockup** — interactive frame with CPL, booked patients, show-rate, revenue attributed. "This is what your Monday morning looks like."
8. **Proof / case studies** — 3 anonymized case bars (e.g., "VA-based clinic, 242-review tier: $148 → $79 CPL in 51 days"). Use the Radar competitor archetypes as inspiration without naming them.
9. **Competitive grid** — LeadForge vs. typical agency vs. DIY ads vs. NexHealth/Zenoti. Rows: dashboard, ad creative, AI booking, no-show recovery, review pipeline, transparent pricing.
10. **Pricing block** — Three tiers: Starter ($1,495/mo + ad spend) for 1-location clinics, Growth ($2,995/mo) for 2–5 locations, Multi-Market (custom) for telehealth scale. Each shows guaranteed CPL band and dashboard access.
11. **FAQ** — HIPAA & compliance, contract length, ad-spend ownership, what happens if CPL guarantee is missed, who runs the calls.
12. **Final CTA** — "Get your 12-month CPL forecast in 20 minutes." Calendar embed, no form-to-call gate.

### Visual direction

- **Color theme:** Charcoal (#0F1115) dominant + electric green accent (#00E08F) for "savings" + warm cream (#F4F1E8) for body. Reads "fintech for healthcare," not spa.
- **Typography:** Inter Tight or Söhne for headlines, Inter for body. Numerical data in JetBrains Mono for "real metrics" vibe.
- **Animation style:** Crisp, low-flourish — counters that animate on scroll, line graphs that draw, dashboard cards that lift on hover. No parallax, no blob shapes. Frame-rate is the brand.
- **CTA strategy:** One green pill button on every screen, sticky on mobile. Secondary action is text-link "see math."
- **Proof strategy:** Numerical case bars; reviewer-style quote cards from "operators"; logo strip optional.
- **Mobile-first notes:** Sticky bottom CTA bar, dashboard mock collapses to single-card swiper, all stat blocks stack 2-up not 3-up.

### SEO/content notes

- Target keywords: "patient acquisition agency for medical weight loss," "GLP-1 lead generation," "lower CPL weight loss clinic," "weight loss clinic marketing software."
- Schema: SoftwareApplication + FAQPage + Organization.
- Outranks PatientGain ("$999/mo") by leading with **measured outcomes**, not flat price.

**Angle:** Expansion / telehealth / multi-location franchise narrative.
**Best for:** Established clinics in MD/VA/NY/TX with one strong location, considering expansion.

- **Subhead:** LeadForge clones your highest-converting location into telehealth-ready, locally optimized funnels you can launch in 21 days.

- **Secondary CTA:** See the multi-market playbook
- **Visual:** A pulsing US map; a single dot in Dallas, then 3 new dots light up in MD/VA/NY along an animated route. Reviews counters appear above each city.

### Section-by-section

1. **Hero** — see above.
2. **Why multi-market, why now** — Stat strip: GLP-1 demand $24B, top-3 clinics in any market hold 56% of reviews (Radar SPD finding), telehealth 0% adoption among current leaders → land-grab window.
3. **The expansion problem** — "Cloning a clinic isn't cloning a Google Ads account." Three risks: brand dilution, untracked CPL drift, scheduling chaos across timezones.
4. **Promise** — A 21-day "city kit" that ships a localized LP, geo-targeted ad set, telehealth-ready intake, and a market-specific review-generation engine.
5. **Unique mechanism — "Market Cloner"** — 4 layers: (a) Geo-localized LP generator (Radar-grade local copy and competitor mentions), (b) Local creative pack (review-sourced ads), (c) Telehealth-enabled intake with state-licensure routing, (d) Centralized dashboard with per-city CPL/CAC.
6. **How it works** — Day-by-day rollout calendar.
7. **Multi-market case** — A composite story modeled on the MD/VA/NY cluster (anonymized): clinic X went from 1 to 3 locations in 11 months, kept blended CPL under $90, hit 4.9★ in market 2 within 90 days.
8. **Trust / proof** — Operator quotes about "we expanded without losing our voice." Map widget showing review volume per city.
9. **Competitive difference** — Versus a generic agency that "launches a new website" — show the difference between building 3 sites vs. cloning one playbook.

## Final recommendation

### Strongest variation to ship first: **Variation 1 — "The CPL Killer"**

**Why it wins for LeadForge specifically:**

1. **Highest match to ICP pain.** Radar SPD #1 pain point: no visible lead-gen metrics across 100% of competitor clinics. The CPL Killer attacks that hole directly. Owners reading this will recognize the gap inside their own business.
2. **Highest contrast vs. direct competitors.** PatientGain leads with a flat $999/mo. Webugol and Avenue Z lead with brand. Goldman leads with a single bariatric case study. **Nobody leads with a measurable, dashboard-first CPL guarantee.** That's the productized seam.
3. **Best fit for paid acquisition.** A CPL-led page sells itself in performance ads — the headline is the offer. Variations 2–5 sell better via outbound, partnerships, and SEO.
4. **Signals operator credibility.** A 35–60-year-old clinic owner who has been burned by an agency reads "Stop paying for clicks. Start paying per booked patient" and immediately converts that into an internal monologue.
5. **Compounds well into Variation 5.** Once a lead converts on Var 1, the upsell to "all of this is part of an OS" (Var 5) is natural.

**What to test first:**

- A/B test the hero subhead on Var 1 — "predictable CPL, CPA, and revenue dashboard" vs. "every booked patient, attributed to a dollar."
- Pricing-block A/B: tiered ($1,495/$2,995/custom) vs. flat ($2,495/mo).
- CTA test: "See your CPL forecast" vs. "Book a 20-min audit."
- Proof-block test: anonymized case bars vs. operator video quote.

### Secondary roll-out order

1. **Variation 1 — CPL Killer** (paid acquisition)
2. **Variation 5 — Operator's OS** (outbound to multi-location clinics)
3. **Variation 4 — Trust Engine** (SEO + content marketing)
4. **Variation 2 — Calendar Filler** (front-desk software comparison ads)
5. **Variation 3 — Multi-Market Scaler** (partnership / event channel)
