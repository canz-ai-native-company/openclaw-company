## 2. Market signals (May 2026, websearch-grounded)

- GLP-1 weight loss market is over **$24B**; **30M+ Americans** on these meds
- Medical aesthetics surpassed **$21B globally**, with **10,000+ medspas** in the US
- Average medspa visit value: **$500–$700**; medical weight loss programs $400–$1,200/mo recurring
- Successful clinics invest **$10K–$50K/mo** on marketing; **$20K/mo** is the typical entry split between ad spend & agency
- Facebook diet/weight-loss CPL benchmark: **~$75**; Google Ads health/medical: **~$125**
- Weight loss keywords cost **$15–$40 CPC** on Google; most clicks don't convert
- Switching from manual CPC to Target CPA bidding cuts CPL **20–25%** in 4–6 weeks
- Audience-temperature segmentation (cold/warm/hot) cuts CPL **20–35%**
- Automated reminders cut no-shows **30–50%** (ADA data)
- Compounded GLP-1 noise post-FDA shortage list and high competition mean **patient retention** is now where margins live

## 3. Positioning gaps & wedges (combined Radar + websearch)

| Gap | Evidence | LeadForge wedge |
|---|---|---|
| No clinic publishes lead-gen metrics | Radar: 0/10 visible | "We hand you a CPL/CPA/response-time dashboard week one" |
| 0% publish pricing publicly | Radar pricing report | Bundled, transparent ROI math on the LP itself |

| Avg site Experience Index 4.2/10; mobile responsiveness 2.75/10 | Radar Design Intelligence | Mobile-first patient capture funnel = differentiator |
| 0% telehealth visibility | Radar Feature Gap | Telehealth-ready lead capture & async intake |
| Reviews are concentrated (top 3 = 56%) but poorly leveraged in ads | Radar SPD + CRO | Reviews-into-creative pipeline (UGC, video testimonials) |
| Booking + reminders fragmented | Webresearch (NexHealth, Zenoti, etc.) | One-stack: ad → LP → AI booking → reminder → no-show recovery |
| Agencies opaque on results | Inbound Medic, PatientGain pitches | Productized, dashboard-first, replace-your-agency framing |
