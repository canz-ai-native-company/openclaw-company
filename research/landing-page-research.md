# Landing Page + Ad Creatives Research

**Client niche:** General & Preventive Dentistry Practice (California, USA)
**Audience:** Local families & adults seeking preventive, cosmetic, and emergency dental care
**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Source reports analyzed:** 8 (Competitive Landscape, Market Trends, Pricing Benchmark, Detail Competitor, Feature Gap, Strategic Positioning, CRO/Content Audit, Design Intelligence)

> **Goal of this document:** Single source of truth so the **landing page** and **ad creatives** stay 90%+ visually and conceptually aligned — same colors, same hero direction, same headline angle, same proof points. No drift between channels.

---

## 1. The Core Problem We Are Solving

The dentistry market this client competes in has a clear pattern across all 8 reports:

- **Reputation is high, conversion is low.** Average competitor sits at **4.93★ across 3,074 reviews** — but **CTA effectiveness scores 3.5/10** across audited sites.
- **Zero competitors publish pricing.** 0/15 show fees publicly. 0/10 have a real testimonial/case-study playbook. 0% offer online booking.
- **Patients drop off** because of cost opacity, unclear insurance, vague hero messaging, and no above-the-fold booking path.
- **Geographic saturation** in California — patients have many "5★ clinics" to choose from. The winner is whoever **earns trust fastest above the fold.**

**Translation for our landing page:** the hero must convert reputation into a booked appointment within 5 seconds — and the ads must promise the same thing in the same voice, same color, same vibe.

## 3. Visual Identity (THE BRAND LOCK — used by BOTH landing page & creatives)

This is the bible. Landing page and every ad creative must pull from this exact palette, typography, and mood. **No deviation = 90%+ match guaranteed.**

### 3.1 Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Trust Teal | `#0E9C9C` | Headers, links, key icons, primary brand surfaces |
| Primary Dark | Deep Marine | `#0B5E68` | Hero text on light bg, footer, dark sections |
| Accent / CTA | Warm Coral | `#FF7B5A` | All CTA buttons (landing + ads), urgency callouts |
| Soft Background | Cream Mist | `#FFF8F1` | Section backgrounds, ad backdrops, soft fills |
| Neutral Light | Soft Cloud | `#F4F6F7` | Cards, dividers |
| Neutral Dark | Charcoal | `#1F2A2D` | Body copy |
| Trust Signal | Verified Green | `#15803D` | "Insurance accepted" badge only |

> **Why this palette:** Healthcare reads as cold/clinical when teal-only. Adding a warm coral CTA + cream background gives the warmth families expect from a "neighborhood dentist" without losing the medical credibility of teal. The accent color does double duty: it's the only thing fighting for the click on both the landing page and every ad.

### 3.2 Typography

- **Headings:** `DM Sans` (700/600) — modern sans, friendly geometry, very legible on mobile and ad creatives.
- **Body:** `Inter` (400/500) — clean, accessible, pairs with DM Sans.
- **Numbers / stats / star ratings:** `DM Sans` 700 with slightly tighter tracking — used identically on landing AND ads.

### 3.3 Photography & Imagery Rules

- **Subjects:** real patients of mixed ages (kids, working adults, seniors), real staff/dentist (no stock if possible). Always smiling, always natural light.
- **Lighting:** warm daylight, soft shadows. No harsh clinical fluorescents.
- **Composition:** close-ups of smiles, half-frame faces, candid clinic warmth. Avoid the "person pointing at teeth model" cliché.
- **Backgrounds:** light cream / soft cloud. Never pure white, never dark blue/black.

### 3.4 Mood Keywords (paste these into any image brief / Midjourney / Nanobanana prompt)

> `warm`, `trustworthy`, `bright daylight`, `modern minimal`, `family-friendly`, `clean clinical but soft`, `cream + teal palette`, `genuine smile`, `California natural light`

### 3.5 Iconography

- Line icons, 1.5px stroke, rounded corners.
- Teal (`#0E9C9C`) on cream backgrounds. Never filled, never multicolor.

## 4. Landing Page Structure (12 sections)

Recommended order — each section serves a CRO purpose pulled from the audit.

1. **Hero** (deep-dive in §5)
2. **Trust strip** — "4.9★ · 800+ local patients · Most insurance accepted · Same-day emergencies"
3. **Services grid** — Preventive · Cosmetic · Emergency · Family · Implants · Whitening (icon + 1-line outcome each)
4. **Transparent pricing block** — "Here's what a cleaning actually costs." (the differentiator)
5. **Insurance & financing** — logos of accepted carriers + "Pay over time, no credit check" badge
6. **Real patient stories** — 3 testimonials with name, age, city, quantified outcome ("Saved $1,400 vs my old dentist"), photo
7. **Meet the dentist** — warm portrait, credentials, years serving the community, signature line
8. **Before / After gallery** — opt-in modal, 6–8 cases (cosmetic / whitening)
9. **Same-day & emergency promise** — bold callout: "Toothache today? We'll see you today."
10. **FAQ** — pricing, insurance, first-visit, kids, emergencies
11. **Location + hours + booking widget** — map, parking note, sticky "Book Now"
12. **Final CTA banner** — coral background, white headline, single button

> **Persistent across all sections:** sticky header with phone number + coral "Book Now" button. The CRO audit flagged "no persistent booking option" as the #1 conversion killer.

### 5.3 Hero Visual Direction

- **Single hero photo, right side** (or full-bleed background on mobile).
- Subject: a real family or a single warm patient mid-laugh in the clinic, dentist visible in soft background.
- Color grade: cream + teal accents. Never blue-tinted clinical white.
- One cream-mist gradient overlay on left third for text legibility.

### 5.4 Hero Trust Elements (must be present)

- **Above headline:** rating chip — gold star + "4.9 from 800+ local families" in DM Sans 600.
- **Below CTA:** 3 micro-trust ticks — "No surprise bills · Open Saturdays · 0% financing".
- **Top right of nav:** phone number always visible.
- **CTA button:** coral `#FF7B5A`, white text, pill rounded-2xl, slight shadow. **This exact button is what every ad creative will mirror.**

### 5.5 What the Hero MUST NOT do (lessons from CRO audit)

- ❌ No vague "Excellence in Dentistry" / "Smile Brighter" headlines.
- ❌ No carousel/slider on hero (kills conversion + makes ad-matching impossible).
- ❌ No video autoplay above the fold (mobile speed).
- ❌ No CTA below the fold.
- ❌ No stock dentist-with-mirror photo.

## 9. Implementation Notes for the Build

- **Stack suggestion:** Next.js 14 + Tailwind. Tokens should match `#0E9C9C / #0B5E68 / #FF7B5A / #FFF8F1 / #1F2A2D` exactly.
- **Hero build order:** static hero first, no animations until after Lighthouse > 90.
- **Mobile-first:** stack hero text above photo, CTA full-width pill, sticky bottom-bar with "Book" + phone.
- **Booking integration:** real online booking widget (Zocdoc / Practice Mojo / native form) — must work, not just pretty. The market has 0% online booking, so a working widget IS the differentiator.
- **Performance:** hero photo as WebP, max 120KB, preloaded.
- **SEO:** schema.org `Dentist` + `LocalBusiness` + `AggregateRating` JSON-LD with the real 4.9 / review count.
