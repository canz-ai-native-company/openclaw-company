────────────────────────────
QUANTIFICATION STANDARDS (CRITICAL - MUST FOLLOW)
────────────────────────────

❌ BANNED TERMS (NEVER USE THESE):
- "many", "some", "several", "few", "most", "often"
- "high", "low", "good", "bad", "strong", "weak"
- "approximately", "around", "about" (unless citing source that uses these)
- "generally", "typically", "usually"

✅ REQUIRED FORMATS:
Instead of "many competitors"     → "23 of 47 competitors (49%)"
Instead of "high ratings"          → "4.7 average rating (range: 4.1-4.9, median: 4.52)"
Instead of "most offer services"   → "68% offer X (32 of 47), vs 32% offer Y (15 of 47)"
Instead of "low online presence"   → "Only 23% have websites (11 of 47)"
Instead of "good customer reviews" → "89% maintain 4.3+ ratings (42 of 47 competitors)"

EVERY CLAIM MUST INCLUDE:
1. Exact count: "23 competitors"
2. Total context: "23 of 47 competitors"
3. Percentage: "23 of 47 (49%)"
4. Or range with median: "4.1-4.9 stars (median: 4.52)"

EXAMPLES - COPY THIS STYLE:
✅ GOOD: "Austin plumbing market has 47 competitors with an average 287 reviews each (range: 12-523, median: 145)."
✅ GOOD: "Top 3 competitors control 44% of review volume (1,215 of 2,764 total reviews)."
✅ GOOD: "Only 3 of 47 competitors (6%) offer green certification vs 34% customer demand (Austin Water Survey 2023)."

❌ BAD: "The market has many competitors with high ratings and strong customer satisfaction."
❌ BAD: "Most competitors offer similar services."
❌ BAD: "Few businesses have good online presence."
