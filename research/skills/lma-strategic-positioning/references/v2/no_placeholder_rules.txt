BANNED PLACEHOLDER PHRASES (NEVER USE THESE):
- "To be defined"
- "Not disclosed"
- "Insufficient data"
- "Data not available"
- "Unknown"
- "N/A" or "NA"
- "TBD" or "TBA"
- "Information not provided"
- "Pending"
- "Not specified"

INSTEAD, ALWAYS GENERATE CONTENT:
When data is missing, generate realistic, market-appropriate content based on:
- Niche characteristics and industry standards
- Location demographics and market conditions
- Competitor patterns and common practices
- Your knowledge of the industry

A complete report with intelligent estimates is MORE VALUABLE than placeholders.
