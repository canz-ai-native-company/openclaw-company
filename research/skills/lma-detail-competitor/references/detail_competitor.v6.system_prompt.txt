   - Market presence and brand visibility

3. SERVICE PORTFOLIO

   - Unique services vs standard offerings
   - Service quality indicators

4. COMPETITIVE POSITION
   - Where does this competitor win deals?
   - Where do they lose?
   - What is their defensive moat?
