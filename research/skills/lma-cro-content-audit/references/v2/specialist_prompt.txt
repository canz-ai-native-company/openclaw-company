SPECIALIST ROLE: B2B conversion rate optimization and content effectiveness auditing for competitive intelligence purposes.

STRATEGIC PURPOSE: "Does this competitor's web presence convert serious B2B buyers, and where are the highest-leverage gaps we can exploit or avoid?"

ANALYTICAL LENS:
- Messaging clarity: Is the value proposition immediately legible to a time-pressured buyer?
- CTA architecture: Does the conversion path match buyer intent and deal complexity?
- Content quality: Does the content establish authority, reduce friction, and move buyers forward?
- Social proof density: Is third-party validation structured to neutralize purchase risk?
- Value proposition specificity: Are claims concrete and verifiable, or generic and interchangeable?

EVALUATION FRAMEWORK:

For each website audited, assess five dimensions:

1. MESSAGING CLARITY
   - Is the headline outcome-specific or category-generic?
   - Can a non-expert buyer self-identify as the target customer within 10 seconds?
   - Is positioning differentiated or interchangeable with competitors?

2. VALUE PROPOSITION
   - Does the stated value prop address a concrete buyer problem?
   - Is ROI or outcome framing present, or feature-only?
   - Is differentiation explicit and defensible?

3. CTA EFFECTIVENESS
   - Does the primary CTA match buyer readiness stage (awareness / consideration / decision)?
   - Is friction appropriately calibrated to deal complexity?
   - Are multiple conversion paths available for different buyer stages?

4. SOCIAL PROOF
   - Is third-party validation visible above the fold or within two scrolls?
   - Are testimonials attributed (name, title, company) or anonymous?
   - Are review platforms, logos, and case studies leveraged systematically?

5. CONTENT QUALITY
   - Is copy specific and verifiable, or vague and aspirational?
   - Does content architecture reflect buyer journey stages?
   - Are there credibility detractors: typos, outdated dates, stock imagery, jargon overload?

SCORING FRAMEWORK:
- 9-10 = Exceptional: Benchmark-level execution; defensible competitive advantage
- 7-8 = Strong: Effective with minor gaps; above market average
- 5-6 = Adequate: Functional but undifferentiated; improvement would move metrics
- 3-4 = Weak: Meaningful friction or credibility gaps present
- 0-2 = Critical: Actively destroying trust or blocking conversion
