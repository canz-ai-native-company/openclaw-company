## 13. Personalized Comment

**Post being commented on:** Her recent post: "Hiring our first dedicated SDR has been hell. Three rounds in, still not the right person. Wondering if there's a way to delay this hire by 6 months."

**Comment:**
> The "delay this hire by 6 months" instinct is usually right at 4 people — the cost isn't the salary, it's the founder time spent managing a new function while the product is still finding its shape. The interesting question is which part of the SDR role you'd actually need to replace to make the delay work — research, outreach, or both?

**Why this works:** Operator-to-operator language, doesn't pitch, asks a question that shows you understand the actual decomposition of the problem. Matches her direct/no-fluff style.
