# Landing Page Conversion (CRO) Brief

> Source brief that this skill audits against. A handoff checklist that turns
> conversion-rate-optimization best practice into concrete, checkable items.
> Two principles run through everything: **clarity beats cleverness**, and
> **one page = one goal**. Items tagged **MOBILE** are the most mobile-critical;
> Section 9 collects mobile-only essentials. Most traffic arrives on a phone, so
> prioritise mobile.

## The 5 non-negotiables

1. The headline states the value proposition in plain words — what it is, who it's for, the outcome.
2. One single primary call-to-action per page.
3. The page matches the promise of the ad or link that brought the visitor.
4. The primary CTA is visible without scrolling, mobile included.
5. Social proof appears early — above or just below the fold.

## 1. Hero & above the fold

- **Headline = value proposition** — in plain language: what it is, who it's for, the outcome. Avoid vague lines like "Welcome" or "Reimagine your workflow."
- **Message match** — the headline echoes the exact promise of the ad, email, or link that sent the visitor here.
- **Outcome-driven hero visual** — show the product in use or the end result, not generic stock or abstract illustration.
- **MOBILE — CTA visible without scrolling** — on common screen sizes; don't bury it under a tall hero.
- **Directional cues** — a person's gaze, an arrow, or a line that points the eye toward the CTA.
- **Passes the 5-second test** — a stranger can tell what's offered and what to do within five seconds.

## 2. Call-to-action (CTA)

- **One primary action** — no competing buttons fighting for the click.
- **Value-based button copy** — "Get my free audit," not "Submit."
- **High contrast** — the CTA is the single most visible element on screen.
- **Reassurance microcopy** — a short line beneath the button: "Takes 30 seconds · No card required."
- **Repeat the CTA** — at logical intervals down longer pages, so visitors can act the moment they're convinced.
- **MOBILE — Sticky CTA** — a sticky header or mobile bottom bar keeps the action one tap away.

## 3. Social proof & trust

- **Proof placed early** — logos, testimonials, ratings, and user counts near the top.
- **Specific over generic** — real names, faces, and results ("cut onboarding 40%") beat vague praise.
- **Risk reversal** — guarantees, free trial, "no credit card," clear refund policy.
- **Trust signals** — security badges, real contact details, and privacy reassurance near forms.

## 4. Forms

- **MOBILE — Minimum fields** — ask only for what you truly need right now; every extra field drops completion — and typing on a phone is painful.
- **Inline validation** — friendly, real-time error handling with clear labels.
- **Tell them what happens next** — a call? an email? instant access? Remove the uncertainty.

## 5. Body copy & layout

- **Benefits first, features second** — "close the books in a day," then "automated reconciliation engine."
- **Scannable** — short paragraphs, subheads, bullets; the page makes sense skimmed in ten seconds.
- **Specificity** — concrete numbers over vague adjectives — "save 12 hours a week," not "save tons of time."
- **Clear visual hierarchy** — guide the eye down one path: headline → proof → benefits → CTA.
- **Manage cognitive load** — fewer choices and links convert better (Hick's Law).

## 6. Persuasion & objections

- **Answer objections on-page** — tackle the top reasons people don't convert — price, setup time, "will this work for me?" — e.g. with an FAQ.
- **Genuine urgency only** — real deadlines or limited spots; fake countdowns erode trust.
- **Pricing framing** — if pricing is shown, highlight a recommended tier and use anchoring / annual-vs-monthly toggles.

## 7. Whole-page & technical

- **Strip distractions** — minimize or remove the main nav and footer links so there's no escape from the funnel.
- **MOBILE — Mobile-first** — thumb-reachable CTAs, legible text, hero CTA visible without scrolling.
- **MOBILE — Page speed** — aim for under ~2.5s — compress images, avoid heavy autoplay video; mobile networks are slower.
- **Accessibility = conversion** — sufficient contrast, legible sizes, alt text, keyboard focus (also a legal safeguard).
- **Motion with purpose** — animation should guide attention or signal interactivity, never just decorate.
- **Localization** — currency, date formats, idioms, and imagery matched to the target audience — not local defaults.

## 8. Post-conversion & testing

- **Consistent confirmation** — the thank-you page and follow-up email match the design and reaffirm the value.
- **Tracking from day one** — event tracking for clicks, scroll depth, and form starts, so testing is possible.
- **Disciplined A/B testing** — change one variable at a time and let the data — not opinions — decide.

## 9. Mobile essentials

- **Tap targets ≥ ~44×44px** — with enough spacing that fingers don't hit the wrong element.
- **Right keyboard per field** — use correct input types (email, tel, number) so the matching keyboard appears.
- **Enable autofill** — let the browser fill name, email, and address; it cuts form time dramatically on phones.
- **Click-to-call & tap-to-map** — make any phone number and address directly tappable.
- **Single-column layout** — no side-by-side blocks that force pinch-zoom or horizontal scroll.
- **No hover-only interactions** — touch has no hover — never hide content or menus behind it.
- **No intrusive interstitials** — avoid full-screen pop-ups on entry; they hurt UX and search ranking.
- **Thumb-zone placement** — keep the primary action within easy lower-screen reach.
- **Legible without zoom** — ~16px+ body text and comfortable line spacing.
- **Test on real devices & throttled 4G** — not just a desktop browser resized down.

## Highest-ROI focus

Design 2–3 variants of the hero specifically. Roughly **80% of the conversion
impact lives above the fold**, so that's where the team's aesthetic talent pays
off most.
