## **Lovable.dev Website Design Prompt**

---

**Site Title:**  
Orquidea Marketing — Law Firm Intake & Lead Generation Systems

---

**Fonts:**  
Headings: Monterchi Serif  
Body: RoboroughCF  
(Backups: Playfair for headings, Inter for body if necessary)

---

**Color Palette:**  
- Deep Emerald (#07b589)  
- Sapphire Blue (#2255ee)  
- Bold Magenta (#ef3082)  
- Royal Gold (#f3b13a)  
- Charcoal Black (#13131a)  
- Ivory White (#fcfcfc)  
- Hot Pink Accents (#ff5790)

---

**Page Flow & Visual Rhythm:**  
- 4 pages: Home, Services, Case Studies, Contact — no layout repeated  
- All pages begin and end with premium parallax sections  
- Unique card/grid logic for each page  
- Alternating rhythm: Parallax image | Plain content | Parallax image | Plain content

---

**Parallax & Animation Rules:**  
- Every image section uses parallax with 3+ depth layers (foreground, mid, back)  
- Text: Fade-up, letter reveal, shimmer, and staggered card/metric effects  
- Cards: Glass/lift/glow hover effect  
- Counters: Numbers animate up  
- Buttons: Glow and micro-scale on hover; subtle ripple/click confetti  
- Carousel, timeline, or sliding mechanics as needed in each flow  
- Never basic motion—premium, spring-based or fluid

---

**Image Style:**  
- Cinematic, premium (avoid basic stock)  
- Lens flares, color overlays, urban and law motifs  
- Always feature glass, gold, or color overlays consistent with palette  

---

**Card Style:**  
- Premium “glass” look with subtle gradient borders  
- Icon accent, shadow elevation, color glow/lift hovers  
- Variably: staggered, vertical, grid, styled tiles (varies by page)  

---

**CTA Styling:**  
- Buttons: Large, pill/capsule, gold or magenta border  
- Glowing pulse and micro-effect on interaction  
- Spacing generous, text bold and inviting  
- Contrasts for accessibility

---

**Footer:**  
- Centered monochrome or gold logo  
- Soft pulse/glow on logo hover  
- Centered compliance/legal text, muted fill  
- Social icons (glass), animated on hover

---

**Unique Decorative Guidance:**  
- Decorative flourishes: digital scales of justice, animated calendar spots, city skylines  
- Section dividers: wave or diagonal (colorful, bold)  
- Use law iconography as linework or divided in background layers  
- Use transition animations (fade-slide/curve, path-follow)

---

**End of Blueprint**  
**Ready for high-conversion Figma, Lovable.dev, Webflow, or similar implementation.**
