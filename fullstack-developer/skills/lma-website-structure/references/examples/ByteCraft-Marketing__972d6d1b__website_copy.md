# Lovable.dev WEBSITE DESIGN PROMPT

**Fonts:**  
- Heading: Playfair  
- Body: Inter  

**Color Palette:**  
- Primary: Navy (#172A4A)  
- Gold Accent (#E5B100)  
- Ivory (#FAFAFA)  
- Blue Electric (#1DA1F2)  
- Deep Steel (#2D3748)

**Page Flow:**  
- Home: Parallax numeric-outcome hero, pain section, system reveal, benefit grid, case snapshots, 3-step process, testimonials, urgent CTA, premium glass footer.
- Services: Parallax service hero, offer/plan table, animated before-after outcome cards, fast onboarding process, floating trust badges, glass-based footer.
- Case Studies: Parallax outcome hero, animated before/after grid, featured "journey" storyboard, testimonial carousel, map CTA, footer.
- Contact: Parallax hero, plain expectations & alternates, animated floating contact form, urgency CTA, glass footer.

**Parallax & Animation Rules:**  
- Parallax on every image section: strong foreground/midground/background movement.
- Fade-up entry for all content, letter-shimmer for numeric outcomes.
- Slide-in card and text reveals, staggered metrics on all grids.
- Card hover effect: glass lift with soft glow and colored outline.
- Animated counters for all numeric stats.
- Button hover: glow + scale microinteraction.
- Section-to-section border accents: animated zap/tubes.
- Staggered, smooth, premium feel always.

**Image Style:**  
- Cinematic, high-contrast, deep navy with metallic gold/blue highlights.
- Depth effect: foreground (sharp action), midground (devices/AI), background (mesh, blur, energy lines).
- No basic stock—every hero custom-shaped to home service/call/board scenes.

**Card Style:**  
- Neumorphic glass, lightly frosted with navy/gold outlines, on-hover glow and raise.
- Icons: bold, 3D, action-focused.

**CTA Styling:**  
- Bright, bold, gold-on-navy buttons, glass hover, pulsing underline when in view.
- Final CTAs: animated stopwatch or countdown/metaphoric for urgency.

**Footer Design:**  
- Centered ByteCraft logo, glass background, soft animated shimmer, glowing underline for nav.

**Decorative Guidance:**  
- Use blueprint mesh, energy arcs, and calendar "tick-fill" as animated accents.
- Zaps/arcs as section dividers; subtle grid overlays.
- Gradient overlays for warmth in testimonial/case sections.
- No visual repetition—each page gets unique hero/composition logic.
