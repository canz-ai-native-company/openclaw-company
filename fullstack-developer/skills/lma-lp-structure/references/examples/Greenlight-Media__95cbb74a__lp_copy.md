## 4-Page Website Blueprint for Greenlight (Cybersecurity Lead Gen)
Design system applied site-wide: alternating section rhythm, premium parallax image sections, modern animations, and direct-response copy aligned to your ad creatives.  
Typography for Cyber: **Clash Display or Montserrat (Headings)** + **Montserrat (Body)** with **cursive colorful accent font** (ex: Pacifico or Caveat) used sparingly for highlights only.

Color direction (Cyber + Soft and Playful): **Deep charcoal + teal + soft neon accents**
- Background: #070A12, #0B1220
- Primary teal: #27D7C4
- Accent neon lime: #B6FF4D
- Accent lavender: #A78BFA
- Text: #EAF0FF
CTA buttons must be high-contrast, glow on hover.

Exactly 3 CTAs across the entire website:
1) Primary CTA (Home hero): **Apply for Your Territory**
2) Mid-page CTA (Home, centered): **See If Your Territory Is Open**
3) Final CTA (Contact page, before footer): **Request a Contract Growth Call**

No other buttons should be CTAs. Use plain text links for navigation.
