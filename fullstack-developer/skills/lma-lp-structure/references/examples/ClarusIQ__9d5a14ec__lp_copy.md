## Lovable.dev Design Prompt (must match the page exactly)  
Design a premium, minimal, modern landing page for **ClarusIQ** targeting **SaaS Founders**. Use a dark tech aesthetic with high whitespace in content sections and cinematic parallax image sections. Follow this exact 10-section rhythm:  
1 Hero parallax image section, 2 plain pain section, 3 parallax proprietary system, 4 plain benefits grid, 5 parallax proof assets, 6 plain three-step process, 7 parallax why choose us, 8 plain mid-page CTA centered and bold, 9 parallax objections with accordion, 10 plain final CTA then compliant footer.

**Typography:**  
- Headings: Montserrat Bold, uppercase allowed for hero and section titles.  
- Body: Inter Regular or Montserrat Regular.  
- Buttons: bold, clear, high contrast.

**Color palette:**  
- Background: near-black charcoal #0B0F14 and deep graphite #0F1620.  
- Primary accent: teal #18D4C6.  
- Secondary accent: cyan #4AA8FF.  
- Text: off-white #EAF0F6, muted gray #9AA6B2.

Flat, clean, strong hierarchy, minimal geometric line icons, subtle noise texture on cards, occasional glassmorphism containers. Use thin borders with low opacity teal. Decorative accents: abstract geometric data patterns, flowing signals, orbit-like visuals in teal and cyan.
